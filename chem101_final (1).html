Gases

Gas pressure · Ideal Gas Law · Gas Stoichiometry · Density/Molar Mass · Dalton's Law

PV = nRTd = P·MM/(R·T)Dalton's LawSTP: 273K, 1atm, 22.4L

⚙ Core Formulas

Ideal Gas Law: PV = nRT
R = 0.0821 L·atm/mol·K  |  T(K) = T(°C) + 273
1 atm = 760 mmHg = 101325 Pa
Density & Molar Mass:
d = P × MM / (R × T)
MM = d × R × T / P
Dalton's Law:
PTotal = PA + PB + ...
PA = (nA/nTotal) × PTotal
STP = 273K and 1atm. At STP: 1 mol any gas = 22.4 L.
عند STP: 1 مول من أي غاز = 22.4 لتر. لو الشروط مختلفة استخدم PV=nRT مباشرة
  • Always convert mmHg → atm (divide by 760) (اقسم على 760)
  • Gas stoichiometry: mass → mol → mol ratio → V via PV=nRT
  • Unknown gas identity at STP: MM = d × 22.4

📊 Ideal Gas Diagram

Gas Stoichiometry Steps:
1➡ mass ÷ MM = moles of A
2➡ moles A × (mol B / mol A) = moles B
3➡ V = nBRT/P

Thermochemistry

Enthalpy · Specific Heat · Heat from mass · Hess's Law · Enthalpy of Formation

q = msΔTExo vs EndoHess's LawΔHf° elements = 0

🔥 Key Equations

Heat equation: q = m × s × ΔT
s(water) = 4.184 J/g·°C  |  s(iron) ≈ 0.449 J/g·°C
ΔT = Tfinal − Tinitial
Hess's Law Rules:
• Reverse rxn → flip sign of ΔH
• Multiply rxn × n → multiply ΔH × n
• ΔH°rxn = ΣΔHf°(products) − ΣΔHf°(reactants)
• ΔHf° of any pure element = 0
Losing heat → q negative. Gaining heat → q positive.
الجسم يفقد حرارة → q سالبة | يكتسب حرارة → q موجبة
Exothermic: ΔH < 0 (يطلق حرارة) | Endothermic: ΔH > 0 (يمتص حرارة)

🔄 Exo vs Endothermic

  • Find heat from mass: n = m/MM → use ΔH/mol ratio
  • لإيجاد الحرارة من الكتلة: احسب n أولاً ثم اضرب في ΔH/mol

Quantum Theory & Electronic Structure

Energy & wavelength · Bohr model · Quantum numbers · Electron configurations · Hund / Aufbau / Pauli

E = hνλν = cn, l, mₗ, mₛAufbau order

📡 Key Formulas & Principles

Wave-energy relations:
c = λ × ν  (c = 3×10₈ m/s)
E = h × ν = hc/λ  (h = 6.63×10⁻³⁴ J·s)
nm → m: ×10⁻⁹
Quantum number rules:
n = 1,2,3… (shell / energy level)
l = 0 to n−1 → s(0), p(1), d(2), f(3)
mₗ = −l to +l  |  mₛ = +½ or −½
Max e⁻: s=2, p=6, d=10, f=14
Three Principles:
• Pauli Exclusion: No two e⁻ same 4 QNs
• Hund's Rule: Fill each orbital singly before pairing
• Aufbau: 1s→2s→2p→3s→3p→4s→3d→4p→5s→4d…
Exceptions: Cr=[Ar]4s¹3d⁵  |  Cu=[Ar]4s¹3d¹⁰ (half/fully filled d = stable)
Cr وCu استثناءات بسبب استقرار D النصف ممتلئ أو الكامل

🔮 Bohr's Atomic Model

Visible light: n→2 transitions (Balmer series, 400-700nm)
الضوء المرئي ينتج من انتقال الإلكترون إلى المستوى n=2

Periodic Relationships Among the Elements

Cations & Anions · Isoelectronic · Ionization Energy · Atomic & Ionic Radius · Electron Affinity

IE trendAtomic radiusEA trendIsoelectronic rules

📈 Periodic Trends Diagram

⚡ Cation & Anion Electron Configs

  • Cations: remove electrons from outermost shell first. For transition metals: remove 4s before 3d
  • Anions: add electrons to complete the outer shell
  • Co: [Ar]4s²3d⁷ → Co²⁺: [Ar]3d⁷  |  Co³⁺: [Ar]3d⁶
  • Fe: [Ar]4s²3d⁶ → Fe²⁺: [Ar]3d⁶ → Fe³⁺: [Ar]3d⁵
  • الكاتيون: نشيل إلكترونات من أعلى مستوى أولاً. للمعادن الانتقالية نشيل 4s قبل 3d

📏 Ionic Radius Rules

  • Isoelectronic series: more protons = smaller radius
  • O²⁻ > Na⁺ > Mg²⁺ > Al³⁺ (all 10 e⁻, increasing Z)
  • Cations: always smaller than parent neutral atom
  • Anions: always larger than parent neutral atom
  • في الأيونات المتساوية في الإلكترونات: كلما زادت البروتونات كلما صغر الحجم
Cl has higher EA than F! F is very small → strong e⁻-e⁻ repulsion reduces EA.
Cl أعلى electron affinity من F لأن F صغير جداً وفيه تنافر بين الإلكترونات

Chemical Bonding I — The Covalent Bond

Electronegativity · Polarity of bonds · Lewis structures · Formal charge · Resonance

EN orderLewis structuresFC = VE−lone−½bondResonance

⚡ Key Concepts & Formulas

Formal Charge:
FC = VE − (nonbonding e⁻) − ½(bonding e⁻)
Best Lewis structure: FC closest to zero
Bond type by ΔEN:
ΔEN > 2.0 → Ionic
0.4 < ΔEN < 2.0 → Polar Covalent
ΔEN < 0.4 → Nonpolar Covalent
  • EN increases: left→right across period, bottom→top in group
  • NO₃⁻ and CO₃²⁻ each have 3 resonance structures
  • O₃ (ozone) and NO₂⁻ each have 2 resonance structures
  • FC = الإلكترونات التكافؤية − الإلكترونات المنفردة − ½(الإلكترونات الرابطة)
Nonpolar molecule with polar bonds: CO₂ (linear), BF₃ (trig. planar), CCl₄ (tetrahedral)

🧪 Hybridization Rules

  • Count electron groups (bonds + lone pairs) around central atom
  • 2 groups → sp (180°) | 3 groups → sp² (120°) | 4 groups → sp³ (109.5°)
  • Single bond = 1σ | Double bond = 1σ+1π | Triple bond = 1σ+2π
  • All bonds start with 1σ. Only π bonds make multiple bonds.
  • Hybridization = نوع التهجين. نحسب مجاميع الإلكترونات حول الذرة المركزية
